See [OpenAI Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/row-terms-of-use/). These are representative catalog visuals, not manufacturer-approved product photography or verified product specifications. The database descriptions, prices, stock, and product records remain unchanged.

`assets/images/furniture-fallback.svg` is the single shared, locally authored universal fallback for every product in Shop, product details, customization, and model-preview loading/error states. The older `sofa.svg`, `coffee-table-placeholder.svg`, and `wardrobe-placeholder.svg` files are retained in the repository for historical compatibility but are no longer referenced by product fallback rendering.

The Milo Coffee Table GLB is a locally authored parametric model built from scripts/generate_pilot_models.py; it uses no third-party asset. Its pilot node groups remain 	able_top, 	able_leg, and 	able_brace.
